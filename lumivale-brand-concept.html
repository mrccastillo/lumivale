The Lumivale approach
Illustrative campaign · Community growth

Be useful before you ask for attention.

01 / Find the context

Identify the questions your audience is already asking. Look for relevant discussions where the product can solve a real problem.

02 / Contribute with care

Offer a specific, helpful answer. Make a brand mention only when it naturally supports the conversation, with appropriate transparency.

03 / Follow the response

Review the engagement and the next steps people take. Use what you learn to improve the message and choose the next conversation.
